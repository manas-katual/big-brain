# Lecture - 1
## LAN (Local Area Network)

LAN is a logical explanation of how big a network can be called as local

**Defination** : - 
- Two or more computer or communicating devices which are in a room or a floor in a building or in a campus if connected are are said to be connected on LAN.
- LAN cannot be defined without its characteristic


**Characteristic no 1 :** 
- LAN should be capable of providing high speed, high bandwidth and high capacity communication.

## Bandwidth

==Diagram==

- Hum humans jab communication karte hai hum jis language ko use karte hai communication karne ke liye isko hum english kehte hai.
- English ko represent karne ke liye jin characters ka istemal kiya jata hai isko Alpha-numeric-special characters kehte hai.
- Alpha yani Alphabets A-Z numerics yani numbers 0-9 special characters jaise #!@ etc.
- But machines ko english language samjhta nahi hai machine ko jo language samjhta hai usko hum binary kehte hai.
- Aur binary ko represent karne ke liye jin characters ka istemal kiya jata hai usko hum bits kehte hai.
- Aur bits two types ke hota hai 0 and 1.
- 0 bit means 0V and 1 bit means 5V.
- Exactly isi prakar se har machine ke har character ka pehle se hi 1 Byte ka yani 8 bit ka binary conversion store hota hai.
- For example mujhe mere pc se samne wale ke pc tak R ye character bhejna hai toh jab me apne machine me jaakar R character press karunga toh mera PC R character ke 8 bit load karlega 
- Agar mera PC 1 sec me 1 hi bit bhejta hai toh pehle sec me jo bit bhejna hai wo hai 0 to mera PC jayega 0 ye bit bhejne ke liye wire par 0V apply karega yani kuch bhi voltage apply nahi hoga
- Jab pehle sec me koi bhi current nahi mila to ye samajh jayega ki 0 ye bit aya hai 
- Similarly dusre sec me jo bit bhejna hai wo hai 1 to 1 ye bit ye bhejne ke liye mera pc jayega aur wire pe 5V apply kareaqa 5V apply karne ke wajah se wire par current paida hoga
- Jaise dusre sec me current milega ye samajh jayega ki 1 ye bit aya hai
- Similarly aise 8 sec baad jab 8 bit aajayenge toh ye samajh jayega ki R ye character aya hai
- Iska ye matlab hai ki wire par to bit chalta hai in the form of electrical signal
- Aur kitna bit hum 1 sec me bhejte hai isiko hum bandwidth kehte hai.
- So, Bandwidth is bits per second (bits/sec) 
- Bandwidth bol speed bol capacity bol ya frequency bol hamare liye (yani networking ki duniya me) sab same hai bits/sec.
- ek hi chij important hai hamare networking me agar me aur tu directly connected hai to jab tak meree bhejne ka speed bandwidth capacity ya frequency aur tere sunne ka speed bandwidth capacity ya frequency same nahi hoga mar bhi bhi jayega tere mere bich me communication nahi hoga
- agar me 1 sec 1cr bit bhej raha hu to tereko bhi 1 sec 1cr bit sunna hoga tu kam ya jyada sunega to tere aur mere bich me communication nahi hoga.
