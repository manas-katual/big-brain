# Lecture 2

**Characteristic no. 2 :** 
Owner of a premises is a owner of LAN to connect two computers on LAN we do lease a link from service provider.

**Characteristic no. 3 :**
Cost of deploying LAN should be cheap. Equipment's used in LAN should be compact and powerful enough to provide high speed, high bandwidth high capacity communication.

**Characteristic no. 4 :**
Size of your campus is size of your LAN. Largest LAN is campus wide LAN.


**Example :**

==Diagram==

- HDFC ki lottery lag gayi
- Hum HDFC me bharti hue hamare manager ne hume task diya
- ja kaleena wale branch office ke andar 40000 employees ko 3 types ki services deliver karke aa voice, video, data
- sabse pehla service jo deliver karna hai wo hai data
- toh hum market gaye 40 hazar pc kharid kar laye har desk par ek ek pc ko mount kiya aur har PC se ek ek set of wire nikala aur data communication ka central device switch se jakar connect kar diya
- yani hamne ek set of network banaya for data communication.
- Similarly jo next service deliver karna hai wo hai voice 
- to dubara hum market gaye aur 40 hazar phone kharid kar laye har desk desk par ek ek phone mount kiya har phone se ek ek set of wire nikala aur voice communication ka central device PBX yani Private Branch Xchange se connect kar diya
- yani hamne ek alag set of network banaya for voice communication
- Similarly jo 3 service deliver karna hai video
- toh hum market gaye aur video conferencing kit kharid kar laye har desk par ek ek kit ko mount kiya aur usme se ek ek set of wire nikala aur video communication ke central device MCU yani Multimedia Convergence Unit for Multi party video conferencing se connect kar diya
- yani hamne ek aur set of network banaya for video communication
- jab voice ke liye alag network data ke liye alag network aur video ke liye alag network aise alag alag network ko hum disperate ( yani seperate ) architecture
- par aisa architecture ab koi use nahi karta reason no 1 wire aur cabling ka cost 3 times jyada ho jayega reason no 2 ye inline device ( PBX, MCU, Switch ) hai yani ye communication ke bich me aate hai

**Characteristic no 5 :**
Next Generation campus wide architecture should be converge architecture it should be capable of carrying voice, video, data, triple play services over single converge infrastructure

**Example :**

- Next generation campus wide architecture ke andar 3 alag alag wire nahi niklenge 
- kewal ek inline device hoga which is switch.
- Switch se har desk ke liye ek hi wire jayega aur wo wire ip phone ke switch wale port se connect hoga switch ke cable me pc ke port se wire niklega aur pc me connect hoga aksar pc me inbuild camera hoga agar nahi to web camera externally mount kiya jayega aur ek hi wire par voice, video, data 3 services ek sath services chalengi isiko hum triple play services kehte hai 
- Next generation campus wide architecture ke andar ab PBX aur MCU inline device nahi rahenge ye [[ON Net]] Device ban jayega
